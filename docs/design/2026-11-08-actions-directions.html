AAction ledger + target

The actions on the left; the marked set, current path, destination, and the selected action’s consequence on the right. The target panel carries the copy/move destination explicitly.

BSingle action table

The target in the band; actions and descriptions in one list; the selected consequence at the bottom. The densest, and the list gets the full width.

CPane mode

The active pane becomes the action list; the other pane stays visible. The most contextual: the transfer destination is never hidden.

State shown: local active, three items marked (1.8 MB), current folder UX0:/DATA/BACKUP/, remote connected. COPY is selected, RENAME is disabled because three items are marked, and DELETE is flagged.

Open question: the sheet shows the marks as the source and the current folder as the destination (mark, navigate, then COPY or MOVE TO THIS FOLDER). The alternative is a destination picker after the action; that adds a screen and more presses.

Not shown: local single, remote single, remote multi, read-only and protected states, CONFIRM, and the text entry overlay.

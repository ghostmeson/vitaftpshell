2ALL DRIVES with seven mounts

Every mounted user volume appears in table order. An absent mount is absent, not an error row; savedata0:, vs0:, and os0: never appear. The read-only mounts carry DRIVE RO; COPY stays live there and every write is refused with READ-ONLY MOUNT (D96).

The right ledger holds seven rows, with the switch last; the left keeps six. UPLOAD is dim with NO REMOTE VIEW, and that reason wins over no selection. The target reads PANE and its destination --; the switch stays live while the remote view is lost or disconnected.

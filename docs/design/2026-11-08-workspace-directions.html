vitaFTPShell — WORKSPACE directions

Three compositions for the one screen the product is built around. Same content, same type, same palette in all three; only the structure of the two panes and the transfer dock changes. Geometry and copy are provisional.

ATwo ledgers

Two equal panels, one per side. The most literal reading of “two live panes”: both trees stay fully visible, comparison is free, and each side keeps its own header. Cost: the most chrome, and the active side needs an edge to stand out.

BOne instrument

One hero frame around both lists, split by a seam, with the transfer dock inside the same frame. The workspace reads as a single console surface. Cost: the two sides feel less independent, and the frame is a lot of outline.

CFocus

One expanded list; the other collapses to a context strip that still names its path and marks. Widest names and the strongest single-task focus. Cost: you stop seeing the destination tree while you browse, which is a real cost for a transfer tool.

State shown: remote active, two items marked, one of two downloading at 42%, server ON with one idle client.

Not shown yet: idle dock, finished and failed dock, SITES, SITE EDITOR, SERVER, ACTIONS, CONFIRM, text entry, loading and empty states.

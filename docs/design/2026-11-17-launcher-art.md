# Launcher and LiveArea art

**Date:** 2026-11-17
**Status:** settled (D103, D104)
**Artifacts:**

| File | Size | Seen |
|---|---|---|
| `sce_sys/icon0.png` | 128×128 | launcher bubble, content manager, app switcher |
| `sce_sys/pic0.png` | 960×544 | boot / loading frame |
| `sce_sys/livearea/contents/bg.png` | 840×500 | LiveArea page behind the gate |
| `sce_sys/livearea/contents/startup.png` | 280×158 | gate thumbnail beside START |

All four are indexed 8-bit PNGs, generated by
[`tools/art/build_launcher_art.py`](../../tools/art/build_launcher_art.py). The
palette is copied from `src/gfx/theme.h` and the type is read from the app's own
glyph table in `src/gfx/font.c`, so the art cannot drift from the HUD. The
installer rejects truecolor art with `0x8010113D`; `tools/check_assets.sh`
guards the format in the host gate.

## The problem

D84 settled a two-arrow mark. It read as a transfer glyph — the symbol any FTP
or sync app uses — and said nothing about files. The launcher is where someone
decides whether this is a file tool, so the mark now shows files.

The LiveArea was still two dark rectangles with a yellow rule, and there was no
`pic0` at all. Those are the surfaces a person sees before the app draws a
single frame.

## The mark

**A stack of files, the front one a document.** Three sheets offset 10 px
diagonally: the back is `C_DIM`, the middle `C_YELLOW`, the front `C_CYAN`, each
separated by a 3 px field keyline. The front sheet carries `VTPS` set vertically
down its left margin in the app's 5×7 face, knocked out in `C_FIELD`, with four
ruled lines on the right.

The stacked offset is what makes it plural; the front sheet is what makes it a
file; the two colours carry the settled lane meaning — yellow network, cyan
local. The mark is flat, with no gradient, gloss, shadow, or border.

### Geometry

- Canvas 128×128, `C_FIELD`, fully opaque.
- Mark bbox 83×83, centred on (64, 64): x 22.5–105.5, y 22.5–105.5.
- Sheets 60×60 with a 12 px top-right chamfer; offsets 10 px; keylines 3 px.
- Front sheet type: px 1.6, advance 13.5, at x 23, y 42.
- Four ruled lines 3.5 px tall at y 46, 60, 74, 88; widths 34, 28, 34, 20.
- 24 palette entries.
- Drawn at 3× and box-filtered, then written as an indexed PNG.

### Mask check

The bubble cut is a 26 px corner radius. The whole bbox sits 22.5 px inside the
tile, so no corner cut can reach it: the nearest cut-corner point is 4.9 px
inside the mask. The mark survives at 32 px as a cyan page with a yellow and
grey edge; `VTPS` is a signature detail that only reads from about 96 px up.

## The LiveArea composition

Composition A, **Workspace**. The page shows the product rather than a brand
abstraction:

| Element | Position | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Wordmark | 48, 30 | px 7, halo and chroma as `src/ui/header.c` |
| Descriptor | 48, 88 | px 2.5, dim: `FTP CLIENT / FILE MANAGER / ANONYMOUS SERVER` |
| Rule | 48, 114 | 744 × 1, `C_HAIR` |
| Local pane | 48, 140, 360×212 | cyan outline at 55 %, chamfer 14 |
| Remote pane | 432, 140, 360×212 | yellow outline at 55 %, chamfer 14 |
| Cursor row | left pane, row 3 | cyan slab 358×26 with field bars |
| Drives | 48, 370 | px 3, dim: `UX0:  UR0:  UMA0:` |
| Access | right 792, 370 | px 3, yellow: `READ-WRITE` |

The shell lays its gate bar across the bottom of the page, so the lowest element
ends at y 382 and the bottom 118 px stay field. That is deliberate: a busy
bottom edge would be covered anyway.

### Gate thumbnail

280×158, field black: the mark at 84 px tall, centred on (140, 56); the wordmark
at px 2.2 centred at y 100; `FTP CLIENT AND SERVER` at px 1.5 centred at y 124.
It carries no data and no state.

### Boot frame

960×544, field black: the mark at 200 px tall centred on (480, 240); the
wordmark at px 6 centred at y 370; the descriptor at px 2.5 centred at y 442. It
reports nothing — no progress, no percentage, no version — because the platform
reports none of those during load.

## What was rejected

Six icon directions were drawn and reviewed: a two-lane sync loop (too close to
a refresh glyph), two plain chamfered panes (says "two panes" but not files), a
single split exchange bar (strong, but not a file), a split folder, a ledger
window, and a folder carrying a window. For the banner: a mark-forward poster
(best at a glance, but it shows the brand instead of the product) and a cropped
ledger field behind a title block (the most distinctive, but it implies a
listing the page cannot have). Both remain available as future states.

## Changes

A change to the mark or the banner composition is a new decision. The geometry
above is reproducible: edit `tools/art/build_launcher_art.py` and run it; the
script rewrites all four PNGs and the asset guard checks them.
